About Me

Welcome to my visual journal.

I’m Nikhil Verma, a photographer and cinematographer dedicated to the art of candid storytelling. My approach combines the eagle-eye precision of a candid photographer with the sweeping, emotional depth of a filmmaker. I believe every project—be it a film or a photo series—deserves a unique narrative voice. By blending into the background, I am able to capture honest emotions, vibrant energy, and genuine interactions that define who you are. Explore the portfolio, and let's collaborate to bring your vision to light.
